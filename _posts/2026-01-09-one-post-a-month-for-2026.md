**TL;DR:** I have a bad habit of hoarding half-written technical notes that never get published because they don't feel "complete" enough. For 2026, the goal is simple: publish one post every month. Not necessarily 3,000-word manifestos everytime. Just real notes from my experiences - whatever I'm building, breaking, or learning.

Twelve posts by the end of December. Let's see if keeping it simple actually keeps my blog active.
